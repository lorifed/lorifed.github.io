The problem

Competitive research is the most repeated, least differentiated part of every new engagement — and the slowest to bill.

What we built

  1. 01

    Five-step pipeline: identify → enrich → scrape competitors → synthesise → typeset.

  2. 02

    Apify actors for review harvesting across Google, Trustpilot and marketplace listings.

  3. 03

    A scoring model that ranks competitor threat by review velocity, ad presence and pricing position.

  4. 04

    Deterministic PDF typesetting so every report ships on-brand.

Outcome

What used to be a two-day analyst job now runs unattended in ten minutes and opens every sales conversation.
