Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what data 21STUDIO collects when you use 21studio.io, why it is collected, and what your rights are.

Who we are

21STUDIO is operated by Lorenzo Federoni. For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights described below, write to hello@21studio.io.

What we collect

Contact data you submit voluntarily through the contact form or the newsletter form: name, email address, company name and the content of your message.

Technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and referring URL. This is used in aggregate to understand how the site is used.

Why we collect it

To reply to enquiries and prepare proposals — the legal basis is the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request.

To send the newsletter, where you have given explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email.

To keep the site secure and improve it, on the basis of our legitimate interest.

Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies are used to make the site work. Any analytics or advertising cookies are only set after you consent, and you can change that choice at any time.

Sharing and processors

Data is never sold. It may be processed by service providers acting on our instructions — hosting, email delivery, analytics — each bound by a data processing agreement.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and for the period required by accounting and tax obligations. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.
